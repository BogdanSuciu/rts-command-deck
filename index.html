    Quick sounds

    Fan-made board. Until you upload the original clips, unit lines are read by your device's speech engine and Zerg sounds are synthesized; nothing is taken from the games. Upload a clip under any key, drop a file on it, or use Load clips to add many at once: a file named after a unit or its line (for example marine.mp3 or nuclear_launch_detected.wav) lands on that key. Clips are saved in this browser, and in your private cloud storage when Cloud is connected.

      Layout

      Copy this to back up your board or move it to another browser. Paste a layout and apply it to replace the current one. Uploaded audio clips are not included; keys that use them will need the file again.

      Cloud sync

      Saves your keys in this site's database and your uploaded clips in its private Blob storage, so they load on every device where you enter the passcode. Clips up to 4 MB each sync; larger ones stay in this browser.

      When you connect a browser that has unsaved changes, those changes are saved to the cloud. Otherwise the board saved in the cloud replaces the one in this browser.
