Clips are saved in this browser, and in your private cloud storage when Cloud is connected.

Saves your keys and uploaded clips in this site's private Vercel Blob storage, so they load on every device where you enter the passcode. Clips up to 4 MB each sync; larger ones stay in this browser.

When you connect a browser that has unsaved changes, those changes are saved to the cloud. Otherwise the board saved in the cloud replaces the one in this browser.
